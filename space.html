The 3D space

On the home page, Cyber lays your store out as a route through a city. Each block of the Cyber space section is a stop; the camera flies from one to the next and lands with the stop centred on screen.

Getting around

  • Stop menu: a carousel of the stops, each with a small picture taken from the stop. Theme settings → Storm → Stop menu position places it on the left (default), under the header, at the bottom or on the right; phones always use the bottom.
  • Keyboard: the left and right arrows and the space bar move between stops; Tab moves through each stop’s links and flies to the stop that holds them.
  • Addresses: each stop has its own address (#stop-3), so links can open on a stop, and the browser’s Back button returns to the previous one.
  • Scrolling scrolls the page as usual; a stop with more than fits on screen (a big batch of products) scrolls inside itself.

3D or page view

The 3D / Page switch in the header lets every visitor choose; the choice is remembered in their browser. Page view shows the same stops as a regular scrolling page, chapter by chapter.

  • Theme settings → Storm → Visitors start in sets the default: 3D flight or Regular page.
  • Visitors whose device asks for reduced motion start in page view. If they choose 3D anyway, flights land instantly and the clouds stay still.

The stops

BlockWhat it shows
Image (Release)A wide banner with a heading, text and two buttons. Leave it empty and it uses Shopify’s Home page collection image (or the first collection with an image), that collection’s title and description, then your store name and description.
IntroYour heading and text, or your store name and description.
Featured productOne product in a circular lens with an info card beside it. Finish picks a variant by an option value (for example Black or 80 cm): the stop shows that variant’s photo, price and stock and links to it. An optional background image or video fills the lens behind the product.
Collections (Series)Your collections as a list beside a scope: choosing one locks it in, with its photo and a strip of its products. Or a cluster of hexagons.
Products (Pieces)A rack: the chosen piece large on the left with its price and stock, the others on a rail to step through. Or a ring of products.
StoryOne article from your blog as a spread: its image, the date and author, the title, the opening lines and a link. Hidden until an article is chosen.
Closing linkThe archive: a portal to all products, with the product count.

Each block has Name in the stop menu, an optional info card, and its own angle. Reorder blocks to change the route.

Hide stops without content (section setting, off by default) leaves out stops that have nothing to show yet, such as Collections in a store without collections. In the theme editor every stop always shows, so you can fill it.

The city and the clouds

Theme settings → Storm holds the scene:

PartSettings
ColorsSky, ground and buildings.
CityOn or off, how many buildings, energy pulses in the streets, see-through buildings.
CloudsOn or off, digital dots (they part around the pointer) or soft clouds, how many between stops, opacity, drift speed, tone, and occasional flashes.
Regular pagesThe dotted cloud backdrop behind every other page.
Outer glowThe soft glow around frames and panels: strength, and text color or accent color. Frame lines: white or the text color.
On phonesCity, clouds and the lines between stops, each on or off.

The animations run only while they can be seen: they stop in a background tab, when the scene scrolls out of view, and for reduced-motion visitors.

Collection pages

Collection pages use the same space: the collection’s own stop first, then its products in batches. See Collections & search.
