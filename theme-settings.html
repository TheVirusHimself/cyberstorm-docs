Theme settings

Store-wide choices live in the theme editor under Theme settings (the gear icon). They apply to every page.

Logo and favicon

The favicon is the small icon in browser tabs, bookmarks and phone home screens. Upload a square image of at least 180 × 180 px; the theme makes the 32 px tab icon and the 180 px home screen icon from it. The logo itself is set in the header section.

Typography

SettingWhat it does
DisplayLarge headings. Condensed, heavy fonts suit the theme best.
Body and interfaceParagraphs, buttons, menus and forms. Choose something easy to read at small sizes.
ReadoutPrices, labels and specifications. A monospace font keeps numbers aligned.
Heading sizeScales every large heading at once.
Uppercase headingsSets large headings in capitals, the HUD look.

Colors

Five color schemes. Each section picks one, so you can alternate dark and bright bands down a page. Each scheme sets background, text, panels, decorative lines, two accents, the button, and status colors for in stock, sold out and sale.

Layout

SettingWhat it does
Page width and marginHow wide content grows on large screens and the space at the screen edges.
Panel cornersNotched corners are the HUD look; square suits a calmer store.
Section spacingScales the top and bottom spacing of every section at once.
TransparencyLets the city and rain show through section backgrounds and panels. Keep panels opaque enough for easy reading.
Breadcrumbs, back to topNavigation aids for inner pages and long pages.

Motion and effects

Section reveals, depth scrolling and brief glitch effects on headings. Glitches never touch prices, buttons or checkout. People who set their device to reduce motion always get a still, fully visible store, whatever you choose here.

Cart

Gift wrap product and Show a gift note field: see Cart > Gift wrap. Leave the product empty to hide gift wrapping.

Favorites

Show favorites puts a heart on every product card and a Save button under the product title. Saved products appear in a drawer opened by the heart in the header (on narrow phones, from the menu). The list lives in the shopper's own browser: no customer account, no app, and nothing is sent to you or anyone else, so it is kept per device.

Badges

“New” shows on products published within the number of days you set (0 turns it off). Tag a product badge:Limited to show a “Limited” badge; any text after badge: works. Sold out and sale badges come from inventory and prices.

Social media

Paste your full profile links. Links appear in the footer; empty fields are hidden.

Categories

Connects the theme to your category tree. See Categories.

Storm

PartSettings

LightningA strike on page change, random strikes, color and brightness. Never more than two flashes a second.
CityBuilding and window colors, opacity, depth on scroll, and your own artwork for each of the three layers.
On slower phones, choose Reduced for rain on mobile. The storm waits until the page has loaded before it starts.
