Theme settings

Store-wide choices live in the theme editor under Theme settings (the gear icon). They apply to every page.

Presets

Three starting points, each a full set of colors, fonts and frames: Cyber (silver and gold, white frames), Exhibition (dark, with brass) and Drop (bright, with frame lines in the text color). Choosing a preset changes these settings, not your content.

Logo and favicon

The favicon is the small icon in browser tabs, bookmarks and phone home screens: upload a square image of at least 180 × 180 px. The logo itself is set in the Header section.

Typography

SettingWhat it does
DisplayHeadings, product names and the names in lists. Every heading takes this font’s own weight. The default is IBM Plex Mono, the instrument-label look.
Body and interfaceParagraphs, buttons, menus and forms.
ReadoutPrices, labels and small readouts. A monospace font keeps numbers aligned.
Heading sizeScales every large heading at once.
Uppercase headingsSets headings in capitals with open letter spacing (on by default).

Colors

Five color schemes. Each section picks one, so you can alternate bands down a page. A scheme sets the background, text, panels, lines, the accent (prices, highlights), the button and its label, and status colors. The frames drawn around panels, hexagons and rings take the colors of the scheme they sit in; the first scheme also colors the header and anything without a section of its own.

Layout

SettingWhat it does
Page width and marginHow wide content grows on large screens and the space at the screen edges.
Section spacingScales the top and bottom spacing of every section at once.
TransparencyHow much the cloud backdrop shows through section backgrounds and panels.
Panel cornersNotched (the HUD look) or square.
Breadcrumbs, back to topNavigation aids for inner and long pages.

Cart

Free shipping threshold, gift wrap product, gift note and the terms checkbox. See Cart.

Favorites

Show favorites puts a heart on product cards and a Save button on product pages; saved products open in a drawer from the header. The list is saved in the shopper’s browser only (the drawer says so): it is not shared with you or kept across devices. Off by default.

Compare

Show Compare on product cards lets shoppers pick up to four products and compare them side by side: price, type, brand, every option and your specifications.

Badges

  • “New” shows on products published within the days you set (0 turns it off).
  • Badges from tags: tag a product badge:Limited to show “Limited”; any text after badge: works.
  • Show savings adds a chip next to sale prices, as an amount or a percentage.
  • Archive tag (default archive): sold-out products with this tag read “Archived” instead of “Sold out” and offer no restock request. See Products.

Social media

Your profile links (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Facebook, Pinterest, Threads, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Tumblr, Vimeo, Discord, Twitch) appear in the footer; empty fields are hidden. Sharing options choose the networks the Share block offers next to Copy link.

Motion and effects

Motion: Off, Subtle or Expressive, for section reveals and depth scrolling on regular pages. People who set their device to reduce motion always get a still store, whatever you choose. Show keyboard hints shows small keycaps such as Esc next to controls, only to visitors using a mouse and keyboard.

Storm (the 3D space)

The view visitors start in, the stop menu’s position, the sky, ground, city and clouds, the backdrop on regular pages, the outer glow and frame lines, and what phones show. See The 3D space.

Categories

Connects the theme to your own category tree. See Categories.
