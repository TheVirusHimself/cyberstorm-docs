FAQ

Can my visitors skip the 3D flight?

Yes. The 3D / Page switch in the header gives any visitor a regular scrolling page with the same content, and remembers their choice. Theme settings → Storm → Visitors start in sets what new visitors see first. People who prefer reduced motion start in page view automatically.

Will the 3D space slow my store down?

The theme’s own code is small (about 30 KB of scripts and 30 KB of styles per page). The city and clouds draw only while they can be seen, pause in background tabs, and can be turned off per part, or separately on phones (Theme settings → Storm → On phones).

My home page shows my store name and the Home page collection. Where does that come from?

Every stop reads your store when you leave its text empty: the Release stop uses Shopify’s Home page collection image (or your first collection with an image), then your store name and description. Fill in the block’s own fields to replace them.

A stop is empty. How do I hide it?

Fill it (choose a product, collections or an article), remove the block, or turn on Hide stops without content in the Cyber space section.

Why are my product photos cropped in the catalogue and on the product page?

The hexagons and the product frame cut the photo to their shape. Photos with the product centred and some space around it work best. On the product page, set Product photo frame to Floating to show the whole photo, or adjust the Photo mat.

How do I feature one finish of a product?

In the Featured product stop, type the option value in Finish (for example Black or 80 cm). The stop shows that variant’s photo, price and stock, and links to it.

What is the difference between “Sold out” and “Archived”?

“Sold out” is temporary: shoppers can send a restock request. Tag a product archive when it will not come back: once sold out it reads “Archived” and offers no request. See Products.

How do I show a product’s price history or how many have sold?

Turn them on in the Price and stock block, then fill two product metafields. See Price data.

Does the theme include a wishlist?

Favorites: shoppers save products with a heart and see them in a drawer. The list is kept in the shopper’s browser only (the drawer says so); it is not shared with you or synced across devices. For accounts-based wishlists, use a wishlist app.

Why does my filter list only show availability and price?

Filters come from the Search & Discovery app. Add product type, vendor, color, size and more under Filters → Add filter.

How do I add dropdown menus?

In Online Store → Navigation, drag links under another link to nest them. Nested links open as dropdowns in the header and as lists in the phone menu.

How do I make a product show “Pre-order”?

Tag it pre-order or preorder, or assign the product.preorder template and write your expected date in its note.

How do I add my own badge, such as “Limited”?

Tag the product badge:Limited. Any text after badge: works.

Where do newsletter signups go?

To Customers in your admin, tagged newsletter (and popup for popup signups).

How do I update the theme without losing my changes?

When an update is available, Shopify offers it in Online Store → Themes. Your settings and content carry over; code edits you made yourself do not, so keep a note of them.
