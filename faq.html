FAQ

How do I turn off the storm animation?

Theme settings → Storm → turn off Show storm background. You can also keep the skyline and turn off only the rain or lightning.

Will the animation slow down my store?

The storm starts only after the page has loaded, pauses when the tab is hidden, and can run in reduced mode on phones. The 3D viewer and models load only when a shopper opens 3D.

Why don’t my colors show swatches?

The option must be called “Color” or “Colour”. Common names get automatic swatches; set exact swatches on the option in the product editor for anything else.

Why does my filter list only show availability and price?

Filters come from the Search & Discovery app. Add product type, vendor, color, size and more under Filters → Add filter.

How do I make a product show “Pre-order”?

Tag it pre-order or preorder.

How do I add my own badge, such as “Limited”?

Tag the product badge:Limited. Any text after badge: works; up to three badges show per card.

Why is “New” on every product?

“New” shows on products published within the days set in Theme settings → Badges. Lower the number or set 0 to turn it off.

Where do newsletter signups go?

To Customers in your admin, tagged newsletter (and popup for popup signups).

Can I show different popups on different pages?

Yes. Add several Popup sections in the footer area and choose different page types for each.

The category trail shows the wrong category. Why?

A product follows its deepest category field. Without fields it falls back to collections linked on the category entries; check that each entry links the right collection. See Categories.

Does the theme include a wishlist?

No. Shopify Theme Store themes can’t include wishlists; use a wishlist app. App blocks can be added to the product page.

How do I update the theme without losing my changes?

When an update is available, Shopify offers it in Online Store → Themes. Your settings and content carry over; code edits you made yourself do not, so keep a copy of any custom code.
