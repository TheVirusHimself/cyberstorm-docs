Cart

Adding to cart sends the product photo flying into the header cart, then opens the cart drawer. The cart page offers the same features at full width.

Cart drawer

SettingWhat it does
Use cart drawerWhen off, Add to cart goes straight to the cart page.
Open the drawer after addingWhen off, the product flies into the cart and the count updates, and shoppers stay on the page.
Order noteA field for delivery instructions, visible on the order in your admin.
SuggestionsUp to three products under the cart items: your complementary pairings from Search & Discovery first, then related products. They load only when the drawer opens.

Free shipping progress

Theme settings > Cart > Free shipping threshold shows a progress bar in the cart drawer and at the top of the cart page summary, in your store currency. Match it to your shipping settings; 0 hides it, and it also hides for shoppers paying in another currency. The first time a shopper reaches the goal in a visit, the bar fills, flashes once and then holds in the success color.

Gift wrap

Offer gift wrapping without an app:

  1. In Products, create a product called, for example, "Gift wrap", with its price and a photo. Turn off Track quantity (and This is a physical product if wrapping adds no weight).
  2. In the theme editor, open Theme settings > Cart and choose it as the Gift wrap product.

The cart drawer and the cart page now show Add gift wrap with the price. Ticking it adds the product once; unticking removes it. The gift wrap line in the cart has no quantity stepper and wears a neon ribbon on its photo.

With Show a gift note field on, a note box appears once gift wrap is ticked. The note is saved on the order as Gift note (in the order's Additional details), so you can print it on a card.

Terms checkbox

Turn on Theme settings > Cart > Require agreeing to terms to add an "I agree to the terms and conditions" checkbox above checkout in the cart drawer and on the cart page. Checkout stays disabled, with a short hint, until it is ticked; express payment buttons on the cart page are dimmed until then. The link goes to the page you choose, or to your Terms of service policy.

Discounts and checkout

Discounts appear on cart lines and totals as Shopify applies them. Accelerated checkout buttons show on the cart page. Checkout itself is Shopify’s and is set up in your Shopify settings, not in the theme.
