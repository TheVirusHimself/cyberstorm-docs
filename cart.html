Cart

Adding to cart sends the product photo flying into the header cart, then opens the cart drawer. The cart page offers the same features at full width.

Cart drawer

Its settings are in the theme editor under Cart drawer (open the cart in the editor’s preview, or find it in the section list).

SettingWhat it does
Use cart drawerWhen off, Add to cart goes straight to the cart page.
Open the drawer after addingWhen off, the product flies into the cart, the count updates and shoppers stay on the page.
Show order noteA field for delivery instructions, visible on the order in your admin.
Show suggestionsUp to three products under the cart items: your complementary pairings from Search & Discovery first, then related products. They load only when the drawer opens.
“Continue shopping” collectionAn empty cart shows three products from this collection.

Changing quantities

Shoppers can change several lines quickly: changes are gathered and saved together, and a change made while another is saving is never lost. Quantity rules (minimum, maximum, increments) are followed and explained on the line. If Shopify refuses a change (for example, not enough stock), the line goes back to the last saved quantity and says why. The drawer and the cart page always show the same cart.

Free shipping progress

Theme settings → Cart → Free shipping threshold shows a progress bar in the cart drawer and the cart page, in your store currency. It is a simple total-based reminder, not a check of each shopper’s shipping address or rates: set it to match your shipping settings, and leave it at 0 (hidden) if free shipping depends on the destination or the products.

Gift wrap

  1. In Products, create a product such as “Gift wrap”, with its price and a photo. Turn off Track quantity.
  2. In Theme settings → Cart, choose it as the Gift wrap product.

The cart drawer and cart page then show Add gift wrap with the price. With Show a gift note field on, a note box appears once gift wrap is ticked; the note is saved on the order as “Gift note”.

Terms checkbox

Theme settings → Cart → Require agreeing to terms adds an “I agree to the terms and conditions” checkbox above checkout; checkout stays disabled until it is ticked. Choose the Terms page it links to.

Discounts and checkout

Discounts appear on cart lines and totals as Shopify applies them. Accelerated checkout buttons show on the cart page. Checkout itself is Shopify’s and is set up in your Shopify settings.
