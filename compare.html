Compare

Shoppers compare up to four products side by side on a page of its own: photos and names across the top, then price, availability, brand, type, every option and your specifications. On by default (Theme settings → Compare → Show Compare on product cards).

Where shoppers pick products

  • Product pages: a Compare toggle next to Save, and Compare with similar, which opens the page with this product and up to three of the same type from its collections (Shopify’s related products fill any gaps).
  • The catalogue: a Compare toggle in each batch’s Selected line, for the product last pointed at.
  • Product cards elsewhere (search, featured collections, recommendations).

Picks are kept in the shopper’s browser. A tray docks at the bottom of the screen with them; its Compare button opens the page.

The compare page

  • Shareable: the products are in the page address, so a comparison can be copied (Copy link) and opened by anyone.
  • Only differences hides rows where every product agrees; rows that differ are marked.
  • Each product can be removed; with fewer than four, a slot invites adding another.
  • The last row adds single-variant products to the cart, or opens the product to choose options.
  • On phones about two products show at a time; swipe sideways, the row labels stay in place.

Specifications

Every product metafield in the specs namespace becomes a row, named after its key (specs.battery_life → Battery life). Create them under Settings → Custom data → Products, then fill them on each product.

Its own address (optional)

The compare page works without setup. To give it an address for your menus, create a page (Online Store → Pages) with the compare template and choose it in Theme settings → Compare → Compare page.
