Products

The product page puts the photo on an inspection stage, with the product’s information on one side and buying on the other. On phones the photo always comes first.

The stage

SettingWhat it does
LayoutProduct in the center (info left, buying right), on the left or on the right.
Product photo frameCircle, Hexagon or Rectangle crop the photo to that shape inside a slim frame; the radar behind takes the same shape. Floating (no crop) shows the whole photo, for transparent PNGs.
Photo matA band between the cut photo and its frame, 0 to 48 px; 0 removes it. Mat colour sets its color (empty: the color scheme’s white).
Show HUD ringsThe radar lines behind the photo.
Sticky add-to-cart barA bar with price and Add to cart once the main button scrolls away.

The stage grows to the screen’s height on wide screens. Several photos, videos and 3D models appear as a chain of nodes under it; arrow keys and swipes work too, and photos open in a zoom view. A 3D button appears only when a product has a model (GLB), with View in your space on phones that support AR.

Annotations

Add up to four Annotation blocks: a short label and value pinned to a corner of the stage (an edition, a material, a size). Connect the value to a product metafield (the database icon) so each product shows its own; empty values are hidden. On phones they list under the photo.

Blocks and zones

Blocks go to fixed zones, so the page keeps its shape whatever order you add them in:

  • Info panel: Title, Price and stock, SKU, Share, Text.
  • Buy panel: Variant picker, Buy buttons, Trust lines, Custom Liquid and app blocks (such as reviews or subscriptions).
  • Below: Detail tabs, Specifications, Ask a question.

Reorder blocks within a zone to change their order there.

Variants

  • Buttons, swatches or a dropdown; options with Shopify swatches show as swatches. Unavailable combinations are struck through.
  • Assign an image to each variant: choosing a variant shows its photo, and choosing a photo selects its variant.
  • The buy panel names the selected variant (“Selected // Black / 80 cm”) and its price.
  • Products with many variants work up to Shopify’s 2,048. On products with more than 250 variants, choosing a photo selects its variant only for the first 250; options themselves always work. The product.high-variant template uses dropdowns for long option lists.

Quantity rules

Minimum, maximum and increment rules set in Shopify (B2B catalogs and volume pricing) are followed everywhere a quantity can be chosen: the product page, the cart drawer, the cart page and bundles. The rule is shown under the quantity (“Minimum 5 · In steps of 5”); a quantity that breaks it is corrected and the shopper is told. With the quantity selector hidden, the minimum is still added.

Sold out, restock requests and the archive

  • Sold out: a temporary stockout. With Show a restock request form when sold out (Buy buttons block), a shopper can leave their email; it reaches you through your store’s contact form (the inbox under Settings → Notifications). It is a request you answer yourself, not an automatic email.
  • Archived: a piece that will not come back. Tag the product archive (Theme settings → Badges → Archive tag sets the tag): when it is sold out it reads “Archived” everywhere instead of “Sold out”, and offers no restock request.
  • Incoming stock from Shopify inventory transfers shows as a dated line when Shopify has it.

Price data (optional)

Price history (a chart of the price over time) and units sold (“128 sold”) can be turned on in the Price and stock block. Both read product metafields your store keeps up to date. See Price data.

Templates

TemplateUse it for
productEvery product by default.
product.preorderProducts not out yet: the button reads Pre-order and a note says so. Write your own expected date and how you ship pre-orders in the note.
product.dropA limited release with a countdown to its date.
product.high-variantProducts with long option lists (dropdowns).

Assign a template in the product editor under Theme template. Tag a product pre-order (or preorder) to change its button to Pre-order on any template.

Quick view and gift cards

On product cards, Choose options opens a quick view with the product’s media and options. For gift cards, shoppers can send the card to someone with a recipient email, an optional name, a message and a send date; the gift card page shows the balance, code, a QR code and Copy code.
