Products

The product page centers the product on a holographic stage, with information on one side and buying on the other. On phones the product always comes first.

Layout and stage

  • Layout: product in the center, on the left or on the right.
  • Product image style: Float suits transparent PNGs; Lens crops photos with backgrounds into a circle.
  • HUD rings: the animated rings around the product; turn off for a calmer stage.
  • Media nodes: each image, video and 3D model is a circular node under the stage; arrow keys and swipes work too.

Colors and variants

  1. Name your color option Color (or the word for color in your store's language, such as Farbe, Couleur, カラー or 颜色). An option whose values have Shopify swatches always counts as the color option.
  2. Common color names (black, graphite, magenta, yellow…) get a matching swatch automatically. For exact colors, set swatches on the option in the product editor; Shopify’s swatches always win.
  3. Assign an image to each color variant. Choosing a color shows its image, and choosing a color’s image in the stage selects that color, so shoppers always add what they last looked at.

The buy area shows the selected variant (“Selected // Yellow / M”) with a swatch on the Add to cart button, and briefly highlights it whenever it changes.

3D models, video and AR

Add models (GLB) and videos as product media. The 3D button appears only when a product has a model, and the model downloads only when a shopper opens it. On phones that support it, a compact View in your space button appears under the nodes.

Notify me when it's back

Turn on Show Notify me when sold out in the Buy buttons block. When a shopper picks a sold-out variant, a small panel asks for their email. It is sent with your store's contact form, so it arrives in the inbox set under Settings > Notifications (sender email), with the product, variant and a link. Write back when the stock returns. No app is needed; if you later install a back-in-stock app, turn this off.

Shopify may show a spam check (captcha) on some submissions; the form then continues on that page and returns to the product.

Badges, pre-orders and the sticky bar

  • Tag a product pre-order (or preorder) to change its buttons to “Pre-order”.
  • Tag badge:Your text for your own card badges. See Theme settings → Badges.
  • On phones and tablets, a sticky bar with price and Add to cart appears once the main button scrolls away (product section setting).

Quick view and quick buy

On product cards, Choose options opens a quick view with the product’s media (including video and 3D) and options. With Quick buy on cards on (a setting in the sections that show cards), shoppers pick color and size on the card itself and add to cart without leaving the page.

Gift cards

Create a gift card in Products → Gift cards → Add gift card product. On its product page, shoppers can tick Send as a gift to someone and enter the recipient’s email, an optional name, a message of up to 200 characters and a send date up to 90 days ahead. Shopify then emails the gift card to that person on that date. Leave the box unticked to receive the gift card yourself.

The page the recipient opens from the gift card email shows the card as a holographic panel that tilts toward the pointer, with the balance and code in the readout font, a QR code for in-store use, Copy code, Print (a clean black-and-white card) and Add to Apple Wallet. Preview it from the theme editor or with shopify theme dev (press g).
