Getting started

CyberStorm is a neon, night-city Shopify theme for stores that sell clothing and electronics side by side: a holographic product stage, an animated storm backdrop and a full set of selling tools, all controlled from the theme editor.

Every setting in the theme editor has its own help text. This guide covers the order to set things up in and the store-side setup the theme relies on.

What’s included

  • Storm backdrop: a three-layer city skyline with depth on scroll, glyph rain and lightning. Every part can be turned off, visitors get a pause button, and people who prefer reduced motion always see a still store.
  • Holographic product stage: the product in HUD rings, circular media nodes, 3D models with “View in your space” (AR), video and zoom.
  • Selling tools: quick view, quick buy on cards with color and size, a sticky add-to-cart bar on phones, a cart drawer with suggestions and a fly-to-cart animation.
  • Discovery: filters with color swatches, load more or infinite scroll, a mega menu, breadcrumbs, a category map and category trails.
  • Content sections: slideshow, shoppable image (lookbook), testimonials, logo list, before/after slider, video, collection collage, countdown and more.
  • Nine languages for the storefront and the theme editor (English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean) and right-to-left support.

Quick start

  1. Install the theme from the Shopify Theme Store and open Customize.
  2. Add your logo: Header section → Logo. A transparent PNG or SVG works best.
  3. Pick fonts and colors: Theme settings → Typography and Colors. See Theme settings.
  4. Set up the homepage: replace the hero images, choose collections for Shop by category and the featured collection, and replace every placeholder text. See Sections.
  5. Prepare your products: name the color option “Color”, assign an image to each color variant, and fill the specifications. See Products.
  6. Turn on filters in Shopify’s Search & Discovery app. See Collections & search.
  7. Configure the cart drawer and conversion tools. See Cart and Conversion tools.
  8. Optional: build a category tree. See Categories.
  9. Check it on a phone, including the cart and checkout.
  10. Before launch: Online Store → Preferences (store title, meta description, social image) and Settings → Policies.

Requirements

  • Any Shopify plan. Combined listings need a plan that supports Shopify’s Combined Listings app.
  • The free Search & Discovery app for filters and product pairings (recommended).
  • Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile.
