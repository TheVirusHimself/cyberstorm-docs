Getting started

Cyber is a Shopify theme that turns a store into a place to fly through. The home page and collections are a quiet 3D city: each part of the store is a stop along a route, and a menu takes shoppers from stop to stop. Every other page is a calm, light layout with the same HUD details. Everything is set up in the theme editor.

Every setting in the theme editor has its own help text. This guide covers the order to set things up in, what the theme reads from your store, and the optional extras.

What’s included

  • The 3D space: the home page as a route of stops (a release banner, a featured piece, series, pieces, a story, the archive) over a city with drifting clouds. Shoppers fly between stops with the stop menu, the arrow keys or the space bar. See The 3D space.
  • The catalogue: collections as stops of products laid out in a honeycomb, with a large hover card for each piece, filters and sorting. See Collections & search.
  • Product page: the photo cut to a circle, hexagon or square on an inspection stage, info and buying panels beside it, annotations, quantity rules, restock requests and optional price history. See Products.
  • Page view: any visitor can switch from 3D to a regular scrolling page; people who prefer reduced motion get it automatically.
  • Selling tools: quick view, a cart drawer with suggestions, gift wrap, a terms checkbox, favorites, compare, bundles and a quick order list.
  • Content sections: slideshow, shoppable image, testimonials, timeline, map, FAQ, video and more for every other page. See Sections.
  • Twelve storefront languages and right-to-left support. See Languages & accessibility.

Quick start

  1. Install the theme and open Customize.
  2. Store name and description: Settings → General and Online Store → Preferences. The theme uses them wherever you leave its own text empty, and the description becomes your pages’ search description.
  3. Logo: Header section → Logo. A transparent PNG or SVG works best; without one, the store name is shown.
  4. Colors and fonts: Theme settings → Colors and Typography. Pick one of the presets (Cyber, Exhibition, Drop) or adjust the color schemes. See Theme settings.
  5. Home page: the 3D space reads your store first: the Home page collection’s image, your collections, your products. Choose what each stop shows, or leave it to the store. See The 3D space.
  6. Products: photos with a plain background suit the cut-out frames; add alt text, assign an image to each color variant. See Products.
  7. Filters: turn them on in Shopify’s Search & Discovery app. See Collections & search.
  8. Menus: Online Store → Navigation. Nested links become dropdowns in the header and lists in the phone menu.
  9. Check it on a phone, in both 3D and page view, including the cart and checkout.
  10. Before launch: Settings → Policies, and remove any sample text you did not replace.

Requirements

  • Any Shopify plan.
  • The free Search & Discovery app for filters and product pairings (recommended).
  • Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile.
