Changelog

What changed in each version. Update from Online Store > Themes when Shopify offers a new version; your settings and content carry over.

Cyber 1.0.0

CyberStorm, redesigned and renamed. The selling tools from CyberStorm carry over; the storm backdrop and the holographic stage are replaced by the 3D space and the inspection stage.

The 3D space

  • The home page as a route of stops over a city with drifting clouds: Release, Introduction, Featured product (with a chosen finish), Series, Pieces, Story (one article) and the archive.
  • A stop menu (left, top, bottom or right), keyboard travel, an address per stop, and a 3D / Page switch for every visitor; reduced motion starts in page view.
  • Stops read your store when their text is empty: the Home page collection, your collections, your store name and description.

Catalogue

  • Collections as a route: the collection’s stop (with its image, filters and sorting), then batches of products in a honeycomb of framed hexagons.
  • A large hover card per product, three hexagon sizes, and 6 to 48 products per page.

Product page

  • The photo cut to a circle, hexagon or rectangle on an inspection stage, with an adjustable photo mat; annotations pinned to the stage.
  • Price data (optional): price history chart and units sold, from product metafields.
  • Archived products: a tag turns “Sold out” into “Archived”, without a restock request.
  • Quantity rules followed on the product page, in the cart and in bundles.

Look and navigation

  • A light, silver-and-gold look with slim white frames and an outer glow; Mono HUD headings; presets Cyber, Exhibition and Drop.
  • The Cyber header: a HUD bar with numbered tabs, dropdowns for nested links, and a Menu button whenever the tabs don’t fit.
  • The footer as a sheet pulled up from the bottom of the screen.

Cart

  • Quantity changes on several lines are saved together, never lost; refused changes go back and say why.

Before Cyber: CyberStorm

1.1.0

New sections

  • Holo carousel: products, collections or images on a curved holographic ring, with drag, keys, segments and optional autoplay; a flat swipe row on phones and with motion off.
  • Loadout (shop the outfit): an equipment screen of slots to swap through, with a live total and one Add loadout to cart.
  • Quick order list: every variant in one table with quantity rules, a live subtotal and Add all to cart.
  • Promo grid: a campaign grid of image, video, product, collection and sale-text tiles with their own widths.
  • Blog posts, Text columns with images, Timeline and Map (no API key; the map loads only after a click).

Shopping

  • Favorites: hearts on cards and product pages, a drawer from the header; kept in the shopper's browser.
  • Spec compare: compare up to four products side by side, including your specs metafields.
  • Command palette: Ctrl/Cmd + K opens search with quick commands and recently viewed products.
  • Notify me when it's back on sold-out variants, Ask a question on product pages, Share, an SKU block, savings on sale prices and an incoming stock line.
  • Variant picker as buttons, dropdowns, or dropdowns with color swatches.
  • Gift card amounts as chips on cards; cards no longer swap to another variant's photo on hover.

Cart

  • Gift wrapping with a gift note, a terms checkbox before checkout, and the free-shipping bar on the cart page too, with a one-time "unlocked" moment.

Header and pages

  • Four header layouts, a transparent header over the first section, a top bar, and a More menu so links never wrap.
  • Popup reminder tab and "hide for signed-in customers".
  • Templates: pre-order, drop and high-variant products; FAQ (with FAQ data for search engines) and about pages; collection landing.
  • A holographic gift card page; a favicon setting; Threads, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Tumblr and Vimeo links.

Fixes

  • The header fits 320 px phones; the footer language selector no longer widens the page with many languages.
  • Filters keep keyboard focus after applying.

1.0.1

  • Language switch in the header.
  • Category map opens at the top, bolts lead to their category, with sparks that stop with the pause button.
