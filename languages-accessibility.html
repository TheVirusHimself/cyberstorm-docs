Languages & accessibility

Languages

Cyber’s storefront ships in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Russian and Ukrainian. The theme editor is available in ten of them. Right-to-left languages such as Arabic and Hebrew are supported: the layout, arrows and drawers mirror.

When you sell in several countries or languages, turn on the country and language selectors in the footer section.

Accessibility

  • Page view for everyone: the header’s 3D / Page switch gives any visitor a regular scrolling page with the same content.
  • Reduced motion: visitors whose device asks for reduced motion start in page view. If they choose 3D, flights land instantly and the clouds and city stay still.
  • Keyboard: every control works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines. In 3D, the arrow keys and the space bar move between stops, and tabbing to a link flies to its stop. Menus, the footer sheet, the cart drawer and dialogs keep focus inside while open and return it when closed.
  • Screen readers: stops, product cards and hexagons have names; charts come with a text description and a list of their values.
  • Animations run only while visible and stop in background tabs. Cloud flashes are soft and never more than one at a time.
  • Color: the presets keep text contrast readable; check contrast if you change scheme colors.
  • Alt text: add it to your product, collection and section images in the Shopify admin. Banners without their own alt text use their heading.
