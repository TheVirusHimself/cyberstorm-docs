Languages & accessibility

Languages

CyberStorm ships in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish, for both your storefront and the theme editor. Add languages in Settings → Languages; edit any theme text in Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Edit default theme content.

Right-to-left languages such as Arabic and Hebrew are supported: the layout, arrows and drawers mirror automatically.

Markets

When you sell in several countries or languages, turn on the country and language selectors in the footer section.

Accessibility

  • Every control works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • People who prefer reduced motion get a still store: no storm animation, glitches, slide changes or fly-to-cart.
  • The storm always has a visible pause button, and lightning never flashes more than twice per second.
  • Color schemes keep text contrast readable; check your own scheme colors if you change them.
  • Add alt text to your product and section images in the Shopify admin.
