Collections & search

A collection page is a short route of its own: the collection’s stop first, then its products in batches of up to twelve, each batch a honeycomb of framed hexagons.

The collection’s stop

  • The collection title, product count, description, sorting and filters on one panel.
  • Show the collection image (on by default) places the collection’s own image (Products → Collections → the collection → Image) beside the panel.
  • Next flies to the first batch.
  • Showcase (on by default, Show the product showcase): beside the title and filters, a ring of product cards over the collection image, with three lists: Matching (what the current filters and sorting return, with the count), Best sellers (this collection with the same filters, by sales) and Recently viewed (the shopper’s own, kept in their browser; shown only when there are some). Drag, swipe, the arrows or the bar under it move the ring.

Batches and the honeycomb

SettingWhat it does
Products per page6 to 48. Each page is split into batches of up to twelve, one stop each; further pages get a Next page stop.
Hexagon sizeLarge (default) and Extra large keep four columns at a big size; a batch scrolls down inside its stop. Compact fits a batch on one screen as a wide band.
Show collection description, Show filters, Show sortingWhat the collection’s stop carries.
Turn left/right, Lean back/forwardThe angle of the batches in the scene; flat by default.

Each photo fills its hexagon (it is cropped, not shrunk), so photos with the product centred and some space around it work best. A small last batch joins the one before it; batches of six or fewer sit as a balanced zig-zag.

Hover card: pointing at a hexagon opens a large card beside it with the photo, maker, name, price (with the compare-at price and saving), stock, type, options, a short description and a link. On touch screens the first tap opens the card and the second opens the product. The batch’s Selected line always names the product last pointed at.

Filters

Layout: Space or List

The collection template’s Layout setting picks how products show. Space (the default) is the 3D route: the collection’s stop, then batches of products as hexagons. List is one row per product. Either way the catalogue is one continuous list: Products per load sets how many come in at a time, and the rest load into the same list (in Space, into a last stop called More products when the camera arrives; in List, at the end of the list or with a Load more button, your choice). Filters and sorting work in both; a new sort order applies with its Apply button, so choosing with the arrow keys never reloads the page.

For the list, choose what each row shows: photo, maker, product type, a line of description, stock, price, Compare and the buy button. Turn them all off for a plain list of names; Compact rows tightens it. Let shoppers switch layout (off by default) adds a Space / List switch under the title; the list then comes from the collection.list template.

Background video

The collection’s opening can play a short muted video on a loop in place of the collection image (behind the product ring, or beside the title). Upload the video under Content → Files, then either pick it in the collection template’s Background video setting (all collections), or give one collection its own: create a collection metafield definition cypher.backdrop_video of type File (video) and choose the video on that collection. Its settings are yours: Show background video, Muted and Loop (both on by default), and Show a pause button for shoppers (off by default). With sound, most browsers hold autoplay until the shopper interacts with the page, so the video then plays muted. It plays only while it is on screen, and never for shoppers who ask their device for reduced motion (its first frame shows instead). Short clips (5–15 seconds, under 10 MB) work best.

Filters come from Shopify’s free Search & Discovery app: Filters → Add filter for availability, price, product type, vendor, tags, color, size and product metafields. In the collection’s first stop a Filters button (with how many filters are on) opens a side panel: each filter as a group of checkboxes, price as a from/to range, and Show results to apply. Active filters show under the button as chips that remove themselves, with Clear all. In each stop of products, the Selected panel has View product for whichever hexagon is chosen, and Show 12 more moves to the next stop.

Search

The header search shows live suggestions as shoppers type: products, collections, pages and articles. The search page lists products as framed cards with filters and sorting, and other results below.

Command palette

Press Ctrl + K (⌘ + K on a Mac) on any page to open the search console. With nothing typed it lists quick commands and recently viewed products.

All collections and brands

The collections list page shows every collection with products (Shopify’s empty Home page collection is left out). A page with the page.vendors template lists your brands from A to Z, each linking to its products.
