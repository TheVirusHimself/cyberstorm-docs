Collections & search

Collection and search pages share filters, sorting and the product grid.

Filters

Filters come from Shopify’s free Search & Discovery app. Open it and choose Filters → Add filter for availability, price, product type, vendor, tags, color, size and any product metafields. The theme shows whatever you enable.

  • Set the Color filter to display as swatches for color tiles instead of words.
  • Long value lists fold away after the first eight with a “Show more” link.
  • On phones, filters open in a drawer.

Pagination

Choose page numbers, a Load more button or loading as shoppers scroll. Filters and sorting are kept either way, and numbered links remain as a fallback.

Promo tiles

In the collection section, add Promo tile blocks to place campaign cards between products (after product N, optionally two columns wide). They show on the first page only.

Search

The header search field shows live suggestions as shoppers type: products, collections, pages and articles. The search results page shows products in a grid and other results below.

Command palette

Press Ctrl + K (⌘ + K on a Mac) on any page, or tap the search icon on a phone, to open the search console. With nothing typed it lists quick commands: the cart, favorites (when on), all collections, a few collections, the account and the products this visitor viewed recently (kept in their browser only). Typing switches to live suggestions. Arrow keys move through either list and Enter opens the highlighted line. With Show keyboard hints on, the console shows its shortcut.

Brands

Create a page with the page.vendors template for an A–Z list of your brands. Each brand links to a page with all of its products.
