Sections

Add sections to any page with Add section in the theme editor. Each has its own help text; this page explains what each one is for.

Header and footer

SectionUse it for
Announcement barA one-line message above the header, optionally a link.
HeaderLogo, menu (dropdown or mega menu), categories button, search field, account and cart. The header can stay visible always or reappear when scrolling up. Add Menu promotion blocks to show promo tiles inside the mega menu. Layout: logo left, logo centered with the menu below, logo centered with the menu on the left, or the menu in a drawer on every screen (phones always use the drawer). Links that do not fit on one row move into a More menu, so long labels never wrap the header. Transparent over the first section lays the header over the homepage hero or slideshow (and collection pages whose image leads the page, with breadcrumbs off); it turns solid on scroll or when a menu opens. An optional Logo over the first section swaps in a version that reads on dark art. Show a top bar adds a thin strip above the header with a second menu (Help, Store locator), social links and the country/currency and language selectors; it is hidden on phones.
FooterText, menu and newsletter blocks, social links, payment icons, policies, country and language selectors, Follow on Shop.
Popup, Age checkAdded in the footer area. See Conversion tools.

Homepage and content sections

SectionUse it for
Depth heroThe signature opener: a background word, a foreground cutout image that moves on scroll, and a text panel. Use a transparent PNG for the foreground.
SlideshowUp to six image or muted-video slides. Optional autoplay always has a pause button and stops for reduced-motion visitors.
Shop by categoryLarge tiles into key collections. Columns, tile shape, image fit, title position, hover effect, badges and subtitles are all settings.
Collection collageTwo to five collections in a mosaic with one large tile.
Featured collectionProducts from one collection. Turn on Quick buy on cards so shoppers pick color and size and add to cart in place. Set Layout to Carousel for one swipeable row with arrow buttons.
Featured productOne product on the holographic stage with all its media (photos, video, YouTube or Vimeo, 3D and AR), options and buy buttons, on any page. Shoppers change options and add to cart without leaving the page. Supports app blocks and Custom Liquid blocks.
Holo carouselProducts from a collection or a list, or your own images, on a curved holographic ring: the front item is full size and lit (with quick add on product cards), the others tilt back and dim. Shoppers drag, swipe, use the arrow keys or the segments; a click on a side item brings it forward. Choose the ring depth (flat, curved, deep), how many items show, captions, and optional autoplay with a pause button. With motion off, for visitors who reduce motion, and on narrow phones it becomes a flat swipe row.
Loadout (shop the outfit)An equipment screen of slots (Head, Torso, Legs, Hands, Gear: your labels). Each slot holds up to six products to swap through with arrows, each with its own size or color; shoppers untick slots they do not want, watch the total, and add the whole loadout to the cart in one click.
Promo gridA campaign grid of mixed tiles: image, video, product, collection and sale text. Give each tile a width (a third, half, two thirds or the full row) and choose one tile height, so a single section can carry a whole homepage campaign. Videos play muted with a pause button, and not at all for visitors who reduce motion.
MapYour shop's address and opening hours with a Get directions button. No API key: the Google Maps embed loads only when a visitor clicks Show the map (faster pages, no Google cookies before that). Add a shop-front photo to show until then.
TimelineMilestones (date, heading, text, image) on a vertical circuit trace that lights up node by node as visitors scroll. Use real dates from your story.
Text columns with imagesTwo to six columns, each with an image (square, portrait, circle or framed with notched corners), heading, text and a link. Number the columns as steps adds 01, 02, 03 for real sequences such as how ordering or a repair works.
Blog postsThe latest posts from one blog as cards. Choose how many, columns, image shape, and whether cards show the date, author, comment count, excerpt and tags. A chosen blog with no posts yet stays hidden on the storefront.
Quick order listEvery variant of one product, or of a collection's products, in one table: SKU, price, stock and a quantity stepper per row, with a live item count and subtotal and one Add all to cart. For wholesale, multipacks and size runs. Quantity rules (minimum, maximum, increments) set in your B2B catalogs are respected. Added to a product template with no product chosen, it lists the product being viewed.
Custom LiquidPaste Liquid, HTML or an app's embed code. Available on every page; product and featured product sections also take Custom Liquid blocks. If code breaks the page, delete it here.
Brand showcaseSpotlights one vendor: a panel with logo, story and a link to the vendor’s page, beside that vendor’s products. Type the vendor exactly as it appears on your products; the products load automatically, so no collection is needed. Choose the product order (best selling, newest, price) and grid or carousel.
Shoppable imageNumbered hotspots on a photo, each opening a product card or a note. The “Image with product list” layout is a lookbook; placed first on a page it is a shoppable hero.
Image with text, Image banner, Rich textEditorial stories, campaign banners and text blocks.
VideoAn uploaded video, YouTube or Vimeo. Plays on click (the player loads only then) or as a muted background loop with a pause button.
Before/after sliderTwo images compared with a draggable divider; works with the keyboard too.
TestimonialsQuotes from real customers, with an optional rating and product link.
Logo listPress mentions, partner brands or stockists.
CountdownCounts down to a real end date. See Conversion tools.
Service benefitsShort promises with icons, such as shipping and returns. Only use promises you keep.
FAQQuestions and answers that expand in place.
Scrolling textA marquee of short messages; can be kept still.
NewsletterAn email signup. Subscribers appear in Customers with the tag newsletter.
Best sellers, Recently viewed, Customers also boughtAutomatic product rows from your sales, the visitor’s history (kept in their browser only) and Shopify’s recommendations. Best sellers can also be a carousel, with quick buy on its cards.

Page templates

TemplateUse it for
page.contactThe contact form. Add Field blocks for your own questions (order number, topic, date…).
page.vendorsAn A–Z list of your brands, each linking to its products.
page.categoriesThe interactive category map. See Categories.
page.faqQuestions and answers with FAQ data for search engines turned on. Replace the sample questions with your own.
page.aboutYour story (image with text), a timeline, values (text columns) and customer quotes.
product.preorderProducts not out yet: the button reads Pre-order, a note says when it ships, and a countdown to the release date (set it in the countdown section) appears below.
product.dropA limited drop: countdown above the product, specifications, and Notify me for sold-out sizes.
product.high-variantProducts with many variants (Shopify allows up to 2,048): dropdowns instead of button grids. To check it, assign it to your largest product and step through each option.
collection.landingA collection used as a landing page: a banner with the collection title, sub-collection tiles and featured product rows instead of the full product grid.

Assign a template in the product, collection or page editor under Theme template.
