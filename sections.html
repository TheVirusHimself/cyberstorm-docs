Sections

Add sections to any page with Add section in the theme editor. Each has its own help text; this page explains what each one is for. The home page and collection pages are built from the 3D space sections, see The 3D space.

Header and footer

SectionUse it for
Cyber headerThe HUD bar: your logo (or store name) with a one-line status message under it, the menu as numbered tabs, and modules for the 3D / Page switch, search, favorites, account and cart, joined by an optional connector line. Nested menu links open as dropdowns. When the tabs don’t fit beside the logo and modules (many links, a long name, a narrow window), they move into a Menu button, as on phones.
FooterA sheet pulled up from a handle at the bottom of the screen, with Text, Menu and Newsletter blocks, social links, payment icons, policy links, the country and language selectors and Follow on Shop. While reading, the handle tucks into a slim bar and comes back when you scroll up or reach the end of the page. Leave the text block empty to use your store description.
Announcement barA one-line message, optionally a link.
Popup, Age checkAdded in the footer area. See Conversion tools.

Content sections

SectionUse it for
Depth heroAn opener for regular pages: a background word, a foreground cutout image that moves on scroll, and a text panel. Use a transparent PNG for the foreground.
SlideshowUp to six image or muted-video slides. Autoplay always has a pause button and stops for reduced-motion visitors.
Image banner, Image with text, Rich textCampaign banners, editorial stories and text blocks.
Collection gateways, Collection collageLarge tiles into key collections, or two to five collections in a mosaic.
Featured collectionProducts from one collection as a grid or a swipeable row.
Featured productOne product on the inspection stage with its media, options and buy buttons, on any page.
Ring carouselProducts or your own images on a curved ring: the front item is lit, the others tilt back. A flat swipe row on phones and with motion off.
Bundle builderSlots of products to swap through, each with its own variant, a live total and one Add bundle to cart. Products that need a subscription or gift card details, and sold-out products, link to their page instead; minimum quantities are added and counted.
Promo gridA campaign grid of image, video, product, collection and sale-text tiles with their own widths.
Shoppable imageNumbered hotspots on a photo, each opening a product card or a note; or a lookbook with a product list.
Brand showcaseOne vendor’s story and logo beside its products.
Quick order listEvery variant of a product or collection in one table with quantity steppers, a live subtotal and Add all to cart; follows quantity rules.
Before/after sliderTwo images compared with a draggable divider; works with the keyboard too.
VideoAn uploaded video, YouTube or Vimeo; the player loads only when played.
Testimonials, Logo listReal customer quotes and real press mentions or stockists.
TimelineMilestones on a circuit trace that lights up node by node.
Text columns with imagesTwo to six columns, optionally numbered as steps.
MapYour address and opening hours; the map loads only after a click, with no API key.
Blog postsThe latest posts from one blog as cards.
CountdownCounts down to a real end date. See Conversion tools.
Service benefits, FAQ, Scrolling text, NewsletterShort promises with icons, questions and answers, a marquee, an email signup.
Best sellers, Recently viewed, Related products, Product recommendationsAutomatic product rows from your sales, the visitor’s history (kept in their browser) and Shopify’s recommendations.
Contact form, Category mapThe contact form with your own fields; the interactive category map (see Categories).
Custom LiquidLiquid, HTML or an app’s embed code.

Page templates

TemplateUse it for
page.contactThe contact form; add Field blocks for your own questions.
page.aboutYour story, a timeline, values and customer quotes.
page.faqQuestions and answers, with FAQ data for search engines.
page.vendorsAn A–Z list of your brands.
page.categoriesThe interactive category map. See Categories.
collection.landingA collection as a landing page: a banner, sub-collection tiles and product rows.
Product templatesSee Products.

Assign a template in the page, product or collection editor under Theme template.
