Conversion tools

Tools that help shoppers decide, built to stay honest: no fake timers, no invented stock counts.

Popup

Add Popup in the footer area of the theme editor. You can add several, for example one for the homepage and one for product pages.

  • Where: homepage, collection and search pages, product pages, other pages. Never on the cart.
  • When: after a delay, after scrolling, or when the pointer heads for the browser bar (phones use the delay).
  • How often: days before the same visitor sees it again; 0 shows it once per visit.
  • Action: a newsletter signup (subscribers get the tags newsletter and popup) or a button.

Select the popup in the theme editor to see it while you edit.

Hide for signed-in customers keeps signup offers away from people who already have an account. Show a reminder tab after closing docks a small tab bottom-left (with your short text, such as "Get 10% off") after the popup is closed; clicking it reopens the popup, and its × hides it for good on that device.

Countdown

Counts down to a real end date and time in your store’s time zone. After the end it either hides the whole section or shows your “ended” message. It never restarts.

Age check

Add Age check in the footer area if you sell age-restricted products. Visitors confirm before browsing; the answer is remembered for the days you choose. It is a confirmation step, not identity verification, so check which rules apply to your products and region.

Trust lines and service benefits

The Trust lines product block and the Service benefits section show short assurances with icons. Write only promises you keep, such as your actual shipping time and return window.

Testimonials and logo list

Use real customer quotes with permission, and real press mentions. Invented reviews are not allowed.
