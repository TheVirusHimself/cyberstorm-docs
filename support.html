Support

Questions about CyberStorm, or something not working as described? We reply within two business days.

Open a support request

Help us help you faster

  • Your store URL (for example your-store.myshopify.com).
  • The theme version (Online Store → Themes → the theme’s name shows it; this guide covers 1.0.0).
  • What you expected, what happened instead, and the page where it happens.
  • A screenshot or screen recording.

Before you write

  • Check the FAQ and the help text next to each setting in the theme editor.
  • If you added apps or custom code, try the page with them turned off.

What support covers

Theme setup and settings, and fixing anything in the theme that doesn’t work as documented. Custom code changes, third-party apps and Shopify account questions are outside theme support; Shopify Support can help with your account and checkout.
